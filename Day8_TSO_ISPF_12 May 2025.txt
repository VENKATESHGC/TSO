RECORD FORMATS:
	FB
	VB
	F
	V
	U
- F (Fixed): Each record has a fixed length, and one physical block on disk equals one logical record.
- FB (Fixed Blocked): Multiple fixed-length logical records are grouped into a single physical block for efficiency.
- V (Variable): Each record has a variable length, with a Record Descriptor Word (RDW) indicating its size.
- VB (Variable Blocked): Several variable-length records are stored in one physical block, with a Block Descriptor Word (BDW) at the beginning.
- U (Undefined): No predefined structure; typically used for executable modules.
These formats influence performance, storage efficiency, and compatibility with various applications. You can find more details on IBM's documentation.
